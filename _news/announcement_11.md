Much has happened since the last update to this website. I have moved from Denmark to Heidelberg, Germany, to begin a postdoctoral position at Heidelberg University. Life in Heidelberg has been great, and the [research group of Astrid Eichhorn](https://pop.thphys.uni-heidelberg.de/~eichhorn/Home.html) has been an exceptionally inspiring and supportive environment in which to work.

I have also been fortunate to supervise three outstanding MSc students on their thesis projects, and 2025 has been an especially productive year.

Going forward, I hope to update this website more regularly and write blog posts about some of my recent works.
